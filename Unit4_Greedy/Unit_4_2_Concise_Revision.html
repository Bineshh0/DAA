⚡ Unit 4 — Quick Revision

Greedy properties

Greedy-choice property + Optimal substructure ⟹ greedy yields optimum.

Fractional Knapsack

Sort by $v/w$ desc → take whole until full, last item fractional. $O(n\log n)$.

Job Sequencing

Sort by profit desc → place each in latest free slot ≤ deadline. $O(n^2)$ naive / $O(n\log n)$ with DSU.

Dijkstra

RELAX: if d[u]+w(u,v)<d[v]: d[v]=d[u]+w(u,v). Non-negative weights only. $O((V+E)\log V)$.

Huffman

Min-heap; merge two smallest; $n-1$ merges; codes = root-to-leaf bits. $O(n\log n)$. Always optimal prefix code.

When greedy FAILS

0/1 Knapsack, TSP, Vertex Cover (exact), Graph Colouring — use DP / backtracking / approximation.
